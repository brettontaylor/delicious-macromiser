Spicy Heritage Chicken Ragù

Soffritto · White Wine & Vermouth · Calabrian Heat · Wilted Greens
15 Min
Prep
·
35 Min
Cook
·
—
Oven
·
2–3
Serves
Ingredients
  • 2 tbspolive oil, extra virgin
  • 1 mediumyellow onion, fine dice
  • 2 stalkscelery, fine dice
  • 2 mediumcarrots, fine dice
  • 5 clovesgarlic, minced
— Protein & Spice —
  • 1 lbground heritage chicken
  • 1½ tspred pepper flakes
  • 1 tspfennel seed
  • ½ tspsmoked paprika
  • 1 tspkosher salt, divided
  • ½ tspblack pepper, fresh ground
— Braise —
  • ¼ cupdry white wine
  • ¼ cupdry vermouth
  • 1 cupchicken bouillon (1 cube + water)
  • 3 sprigsfresh thyme
  • 1 sprigfresh rosemary
  • 3 leavesfresh sage
— Greens & Finish —
  • 1 mediumgreen zucchini, ½" half-moons
  • 1 bunchlacinato kale, stems removed, rough chop
  • 3 cupsbaby spinach
  • 2 tbspunsalted butter
  • ¼ cupflat-leaf parsley, torn, to finish
Method
  1. 1
    Build the Soffritto
    Heat olive oil in a wide Dutch oven over medium. Add onion, celery, and carrot with a pinch of salt. Cook 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until completely soft and just beginning to caramelize at the edges. Do not rush — this is the flavor foundation.
  2. 2
    Bloom Spice & Garlic
    Push soffritto to the edges. Add garlic, red pepper flakes, fennel seed, and smoked paprika to the center. Cook 60 seconds, stirring constantly, until intensely fragrant. Fold into the vegetables.
  3. 3
    Sear the Chicken
    Add ground chicken in a single even layer. Let it sit undisturbed 2 minutes to develop color and build fond. Break into rough pieces and cook through, 3–4 minutes more. Season with remaining salt and black pepper.
  4. 4
    Deglaze — Wine & Vermouth
    Pour in white wine and vermouth together. Scrape every bit of fond from the pan bottom. Let reduce by half, about 2 minutes, until the sharp alcohol edge softens into something round and fragrant.
  5. 5
    Braise & Reduce
    Add bouillon, thyme, rosemary, and sage sprigs. Bring to a bare simmer and cook uncovered 12–15 minutes until liquid reduces to a loose, glossy sauce that coats the chicken. Discard herb sprigs.
  6. 6
    Greens — Two Stages
    Add zucchini and kale, stir to coat, cover 3 minutes until kale is wilted and zucchini is barely tender. Uncover, add spinach, stir just until collapsed — about 60 seconds. Do not overcook.
  7. 7
    Mount & Finish
    Pull from heat. Add butter and torn parsley, stirring until butter is fully melted and emulsified. Taste and adjust salt. The ragù should be spicy, herbal, and loose enough to pool slightly around the rice.
Chef's Note
Heritage chicken is leaner than commodity birds — crowd the pan in Step 3 and it will steam, not sear, and you will never build the fond. Use the widest pan you have and give it those two full minutes undisturbed. The fond is the entire ragù.

Sides & Plating

Rice, Herb Oil & Service

Jasmine already cooked · Steam to revive · Finish with parsley oil
Steamed Jasmine Rice — Reheat
  • 2–3 cupscooked jasmine rice
  • 3 tbspwater
  • 1 tbspunsalted butter (optional)
  • pinchkosher salt
  1. 1
    Steam to Revive
    Add rice and water to a small saucepan. Cover tightly. Heat over low 4–5 minutes until steam lifts the grains apart. Do not stir or uncover early.
  2. 2
    Fluff & Hold
    Remove from heat. Add butter and salt, fluff gently with a fork. Keep covered until plating — the carry-heat will keep it warm for 8–10 minutes.
Parsley Herb Finishing Oil
  • ⅓ cupflat-leaf parsley, very finely chopped
  • 1 clovegarlic, grated on microplane
  • 3 tbspolive oil, extra virgin
  • ¼ tspred pepper flakes
  • pinchflaky sea salt
  1. 1
    Combine & Rest
    Stir all ingredients in a small bowl. Let sit at least 5 minutes for the garlic to mellow and the herbs to bleed into the oil. Do not heat — this is a raw condiment.
  2. 2
    Taste & Time
    Adjust salt. The oil should taste bright, grassy, with a back hum of heat. Spoon on at the very last moment — it wilts if it sits on hot ragù too long.
To Plate
Pack rice firmly into a small bowl or ring mold and invert at the center of a wide, shallow bowl — the mound gives the ragù something to lean against. Spoon ragù generously around and partly over the base, letting the greens drape naturally over the rice. Finish with a slow, deliberate spoonful of herb oil across the top and a confident crack of black pepper. Serve immediately — this dish does not wait once plated.
Make Ahead
Ragù keeps 3 days refrigerated. Cook through Step 5 (after the braise), cool, and store. When reheating over medium-low with a splash of water, add the zucchini and kale fresh — they only need those 3 minutes. Spinach should never be stored in the ragù; always wilt it in at the last moment or it turns grey and loses all texture.
Wine Pairing
The spice and richness here want a wine with body and real tension. Reach for a Côtes du Rhône blanc, a Grüner Veltliner with some weight, or an Alsatian Pinot Gris. For red, a cool-climate Gamay — Beaujolais-Villages or Mâcon — has the freshness to cut through the fennel and butter. The dry vermouth you cooked with is also excellent served very cold alongside.
