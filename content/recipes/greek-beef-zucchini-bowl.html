Greek-ish Beef & Zucchini Bowl

Ingredients
— Beef & Veg —
  • 1 lbground beef (80/20)
  • 2 mediumzucchini, sliced into half-moons (¼ in thick)
  • 8 ozcremini mushrooms, quartered
  • 1 mediumyellow onion, small dice
  • 4 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 1½ tbspolive oil
— Season & Finish —
  • ½ tsppoultry herb blend (thyme, rosemary, sage)
  • ¼ tspcayenne pepper (or to taste)
  • ¼ cupfresh dill, roughly chopped
  • ¼ cupflat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
  • 1 tspkosher salt, divided
  • ½ lemonjuiced (optional finishing squeeze)
— Rice Base —
  • 1 cupjasmine rice
  • 1½ cupswater
  • ½ tspkosher salt
  • 1 tspbutter or olive oil
Method
  1. 1
    Start the Rice
    Combine 1 cup jasmine rice, 1½ cups water, ½ tsp salt, and 1 tsp butter in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to the lowest setting, cover tightly, and cook 15 minutes. Remove from heat and let steam, covered, for 5 more minutes. Do not lift the lid.
  2. 2
    Brown the Beef
    Heat a large skillet over medium-high. Add 1 lb ground beef with ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp poultry herbs, and ¼ tsp cayenne. Break into crumbles and cook undisturbed for 2–3 minutes to build a crust, then stir. Continue cooking 4–5 minutes until deep brown throughout. Remove beef with a slotted spoon; leave the rendered fat in the pan.
  3. 3
    Sweat the Aromatics
    Reduce heat to medium. Add 1½ tbsp olive oil if the pan looks dry. Add the diced onion and cook 4–5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and translucent. Add 4 cloves minced garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant — do not let it color.
  4. 4
    Sear the Mushrooms
    Add 8 oz quartered cremini mushrooms in a single layer. Resist stirring — let them cook undisturbed 3 minutes to achieve golden color. Season with a pinch of salt, then stir and cook 2–3 minutes more until moisture has evaporated and edges are browned.
  5. 5
    Add the Zucchini
    Add the 2 medium zucchini (sliced ¼ in thick). Season with the remaining ½ tsp salt. Cook 3–4 minutes, turning once, until just tender with slight color at the edges. Pull the pan off heat before the zucchini goes fully soft — residual heat will carry it.
  6. 6
    Finish & Herb
    Return the browned beef to the pan. Toss everything together over medium heat for 1 minute to marry flavors. Remove from heat. Fold in ¼ cup each of fresh dill and flat-leaf parsley. Squeeze ½ lemon over the top if using. Taste and adjust salt and cayenne.
Chef's Note
Dill is the whole point of this dish — add it off heat and generously. If you stir it in while the pan is still hot, the volatile oils cook off and you lose the bright, grassy character that makes this feel Greek rather than generic. The lemon squeeze at the end is optional but strongly recommended; it ties the dill to everything else.

Sides & Serving

Garlic Herb Rice (Optional Upgrade)
  • 1 tbspbutter
  • 2 clovesgarlic, minced
  • 2 tbspflat-leaf parsley, fine chop
  • —cooked jasmine rice (from Page 1)
  1. 1
    Toast & Toss
    Melt 1 tbsp butter in a small saucepan over medium. Add 2 cloves minced garlic and cook 30 seconds. Add the steamed rice and fold to coat. Remove from heat and stir in 2 tbsp chopped parsley. Serve immediately.
Quick Wilted Kale
  • 4 cupslacinato kale, stems removed, rough chop
  • 2 clovesgarlic, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbspolive oil
  • pinchcayenne
  • —salt and lemon to finish
  1. 1
    Wilt in the Residual Pan
    After removing the beef mixture, return the skillet to medium heat with 1 tbsp olive oil. Add 2 cloves sliced garlic and a pinch of cayenne; cook 30 seconds. Add 4 cups chopped lacinato kale with a splash of water. Cover 2 minutes until wilted. Season with salt and a squeeze of lemon.
To Plate
Spoon the garlic rice into a wide, shallow bowl — enough to form a generous base. Ladle the beef and zucchini mixture directly over the center, letting some of the pan juices soak into the rice. Tuck a small nest of wilted kale alongside if using. Finish with a few fronds of fresh dill and a thin drizzle of olive oil over the top. Serve immediately — the zucchini softens as it sits and loses its edge.
Make Ahead
The beef and vegetable mixture holds refrigerated for up to 3 days — store it separately from the rice. To reheat, warm in a dry skillet over medium with 1–2 tbsp of water to revive moisture. Add fresh herbs after reheating, never before. Rice reheats well in the microwave with a damp paper towel over the top (1–2 minutes).
Wine Pairing
The dill and cayenne call for something with brightness and a touch of grip. A chilled Gamay (Beaujolais-Villages or a Loire Gamay) handles the herbal note without fighting the spice. For white: Greek Assyrtiko is a natural match — its saline, citrus character mirrors the lemon and dill directly. Avoid heavy reds; they'll bury the freshness of the herbs.
